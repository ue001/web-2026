Jihae Cha, based in the Netherlands

Jihae loves technology, motion and conceptual design, and her work lives where the three meet.

She is a digital designer from South Korea, based in The Hague. During and after her Interactive Media Design studies at KABK, she followed her curiosity through studios in Berlin, Amsterdam and Utrecht, exploring AR/VR, 3D, photogrammetry, UX/UI, interaction and motion.

Along the way, she found what she loves most, design that moves and interacts with intention. In whatever digital form, she pictures where each element comes in and leaves, and asks why it moves and what story it tells.

Living between cultures, she noticed how differently people keep their space. In her work, she uses proxemics both as a subject and as a design method.
